About the Tragedy of the Commons

The Tragedy of the Commons, described by Garrett Hardin (1968), occurs when individuals acting in self-interest deplete a shared resource. Each farmer benefits fully from adding a cow, but the cost (degraded pasture) is shared by all. Rational individual behavior leads to collective ruin.

Solutions include regulation (enforced quotas), privatization (divide the commons), or community governance (Elinor Ostrom's Nobel Prize work — self-managed communal rules). Pure rational self-interest without communication tends to deplete shared resources.
